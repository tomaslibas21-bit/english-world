# Briefs: the 47 conversations still to write

One brief per conversation of Maple Harbor that Puerto Colibrí still needs (the café, the mercado and the taxi are
done: they are the models; the hotel, s68, was the trial). Each keeps its English file's id, steps, intents and twists unless the brief says
otherwise; the brief says what changes for a Mexican Caribbean town, who the person is, and what must be in it.
Read `docs/es/AUTHORING-ES.md` and `docs/es/STYLE.md` first. "M:" = a Mexican-only word that needs a neutral twin.

Batches (docs/es/PLAN.md): **A** the trial · **B** arrival and getting around · **C** town services ·
**D** home and neighbors · **E** social · **F** work · **G** the advanced chapters.

## Chapter 1 · Arrival (batch B)

**s65a-passport · "¿Negocios o placer?"** — Oficial Ramírez (f), Agente de migración, usted. The airport's
immigration desk (Instituto Nacional de Migración style, made-up name). Mexico now gives most visitors a stamp with
the days allowed (up to 180) instead of the old paper FMM: "¿Cuántos días se va a quedar?", "¿Motivo de su viaje?",
"¿Dónde se va a hospedar?", "¿Trae su pase de abordar / boleto de regreso?". The twist: she gives fewer days than
asked and the learner asks politely for more. Numbers: days, dates. Accept "turismo", "de vacaciones", "por
trabajo", "vine a vivir aquí" (a mover: "¿Tiene visa de residente?" — a residente temporal card).

**s65b-baggage · "Mi maleta no llegó"** — Itzel (f), baggage services, usted. Lost luggage form: flight number
(letters + digits: "AM 123" said "a, eme, ciento veintitrés"), describing the bag (color, size, brand, "con
ruedas", "de tela / rígida"), the hotel address, a phone number (digits in pairs). The twist: the bag is in Cancún
and comes "mañana en el primer vuelo". Teaches maleta / equipaje / reclamo de equipaje.

**s66-taxi** — done (model).

**s68-hotel · "¿Puedo salir más tarde?"** — Fernanda (f), recepción, Hotel Mar Azul, usted. Check-in: the
reservation name (spelling), ID ("¿Me presta su pasaporte?"), the room ("habitación doble / sencilla", "con vista al
mar"), breakfast hours ("de siete a diez y media"), the Wi-Fi, the "late check-out" ("¿Puedo dejar la habitación más
tarde?" — the word "check-out" is used in Mexico too, accept it). Mexico: a "depósito" or a card imprint for
incidentals, the "impuesto de hospedaje" (lodging tax) added to the bill. Twist: the room isn't ready, "regrese a
las tres". Numbers: room numbers, times, nights.

## Chapter 3 · Services (batch C)

**s74-clothes · "¿Me lo puedo probar?"** — Valeria (f), Boutique Hilos, usted. Sizes in Mexico: "chica, mediana,
grande" (CH, M, G) and numbers for shoes (Mexican sizes: 25 = US 7 women's): teach "¿Qué número calza?". The
fitting room ("el probador"), "¿Me queda bien?", "me queda grande / chico", colors with agreement ("la blusa
blanca"), the sale ("¡Todo al 30%!"), paying, "¿Tiene en otro color?". Twist: the size is out of stock.

**s75-pharmacy · "Algo para el dolor de cabeza"** — Don Héctor (m), Farmacia del Puerto, usted. Mexico: many
medicines over the counter, antibiotics only with a prescription ("receta"); the pharmacy's doctor next door
("consultorio", a consultation for a small fee — a realistic twist); generics ("¿El de patente o el genérico?").
Symptoms with "me duele", "tengo tos / fiebre / gripa (M: gripa → gripe)". Dosage: "una cada ocho horas".

**s76-dentist-call · "¿Cómo se escribe?"** — Lorena (f), dental office reception, on the phone, usted. Booking an
appointment: name and surname spelled (Spanish letter names: "be grande", "doble ve"), date and time ("el jueves a
las cuatro y media"), phone number in pairs, "¿Es su primera vez con nosotros?". Twist: the only slot is "pasado
mañana a las nueve".

**s77-salon · "Nada más las puntas"** — Paty (f), Estética Tijeras, usted (or tú if the owner wants a young casual
stylist — the English Jessie is formal). "Un corte", "nada más despuntar / las puntas", "el fleco" (bangs), "más

**s78-bank · "El cajero se tragó mi tarjeta"** — Ricardo (m), Banco del Puerto, usted. For movers and visitors:

**s79-post · "¿Dónde está mi paquete?"** — Gloria (f), post office, usted. Mexico: the tracking number ("número de

**s80-support-call · "Su llamada es muy importante"** — Karla (f) and the NetWave phone menu (a recorded voice:

**s81-apartment · "¿Están incluidos los servicios?"** — Don Ernesto (m), landlord, usted. A mover renting a

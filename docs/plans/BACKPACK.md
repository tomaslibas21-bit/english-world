# Backpack · The learner's field notebook

Open the pack, pull out a coffee-stained card and hear the sentence you chose to keep. Beside it sits the little cup you received for finishing the café conversation. A review takes as little as one card. Closing the notebook returns you to the same spot in town.

**Scope.** Maple Harbor (MH, `glow8/mh`) uses `~/fluent-steps` (EM). Puerto Colibrí (PC, read-only reference `~/claude-work/review2-pc`) uses `~/spanish-master-english` (SME) or `~/spanish-master` (SM). Build later in game implementation trees. $0, existing voices and icons, course data read-only, no downloads, paid media or Worker edits. New recording access **needs the owner**. This feature works independently of town hall, the proposed library and karaoke.

## Where it opens

The player already wears the pack. `src/game/Game.ts` builds the player with `withBackpack` from `src/game/characters/index.ts` to contrast navy or mustard fabric with the chosen top. Carry the same flap, stitching and pocket shape into a 44 px button. Reuse the existing Phrasebook slot in `src/ui/Hud.tsx`, its drawer position and due badge. Label it **Backpack**. A tap opens the notebook anywhere the ordinary phrasebook is available. Keep the panel ID `phrasebook` so old callers, pad scopes and the picture-mode tool in `src/ui/LightHome.tsx` still reach it. The icon also works when the walking camera hides the bag. The small moving mesh needs no tap target.

**Two real places to try it.** In both `src/game/world/layout.ts` files the visitor building is at (−56, −22), faces south and opens at (−56, −16). Walk east along the frontage to the public bench at **(−48, −17.5)**, then open the pack from the menu. `src/game/world/town.ts` places this bench in the street furniture loop in both towns. The PC church exclusion is over by (52, −41). In MH the walk starts at the Visitor Center on Main Street. In PC it starts at Información Turística on avenida Juárez. Use the bench as a review frame and optional seat, with zero new prop, map pin or proximity requirement. Opening or closing the pack preserves sitting or standing.

**A real card.** After saving the whole Friday question in `s83-invite-call`, MH shows “Are you free on Friday?” / “Ar turi laiko penktadienį?” in the friend register. In PC the card reads “¿Estás libre el viernes?” / “Are you free on Friday?” (SME) or “Ar esi laisva penktadienį?” (SM). Keep the exact helper in the saved record. If a saved line came from another supported variety, preserve its wording. Use the Mexican recording only when it matches the written target. In-game cards, due dates and source labels come from the active origin. Never infer the helper language from the name `lt`.

**Two ways to review.** Browse any page, tap to reveal a meaning and listen at will. Browsing, revealing and listening leave the schedule alone. **Review due cards** enters the existing helper-first round in `Cards.tsx`. The learner recalls the target, reveals, then chooses “Got it” or “Again.” Reuse `dueCards`, `answerCard` and the existing 20-card limit, five boxes and 1/2/4/8/16 calendar-day intervals. A failed card can return within the round, with only its first grade applied. Preserve immediate grades on early exit and skip cards removed in another tab. Show the next due date when the queue is empty. Closing and reopening returns to the last leaf in this session.

**Tap to hear.** Existing `Cards.tsx` calls `audio.play` with coach voice and the card text or its saved spoken form. For *all notebook playback*, including that reused review, use the active edition's existing `src/game/audio.ts::hasClip, play`, `src/content/voices.ts` and `src/audio-key.ts`. After manifest readiness, check the exact text, voice and authored speed, then request `recordingOnly: true`. A whole sentence may have a distinct `sentence.say`, especially when it contains a number. A unit may have no standalone take. In that case offer **Hear source sentence** only if the whole matching sentence has a clip. If neither resolves, show the text and “Audio unavailable.” For a source lacking an identified voice use an exact coach take when available. A legacy card stores no speaker or clip ID. Do not guess one from the NPC's name or a localized source label.

For the Friday example, the female/male coach at speed 1 resolves to MH `5fb44353be01` / `e6086c3c84ee` in `public/audio-v2/manifest.json`, PC `5642f36306d3` / `a48b409a5aba` in `public/audio-es/manifest.json`. Those keys establish a catalog match. Audio still needs a local listening check. Use the game's existing authorized clip resolver. On a missing recording, denied access or a new selection, cancel the pending attempt and keep the page usable. Release the foreground voice on hide, close or route change. Pass `optional: true` with `recordingOnly: true` so failed playback resolves and the notebook can offer Retry without invoking a device voice. No speech generation, autoplay on focus or helper speaker without a verified helper recording.

**Cards in the course.** MH uses `english-world-cards-v1` and `english-world-progress-v1`. Both PC profiles use `puerto-colibri-cards-v1` and `puerto-colibri-progress-v1` on their own origins. Use normal `parseCards/loadCards` and the existing store's storage events. Handle old records through the normal parser and denied storage through the in-memory store. An additional save of the same normalized target keeps the original card ID, box, due date and source.

The game exporters `tools/export-fluent-steps.ts` and PC `tools/export-sme.ts` use `VITE_CARDS_TARGET=fluentsteps`. In these builds ordinary saves already mirror new entries to the current origin's `fluentsteps.songcards.v1`, deck `words`, via `src/state/cards.ts::mirrorToFluentSteps`. The course owns its schedule and deletion independently. The notebook reuses this save action, reads its own game deck and performs zero bulk imports, resyncs or mutations on open.

On the saved-cards leaf use **Open course cards**. Link to the learner's own collection on the configured enclosing course origin, EM or SM **`/korteles?mode=mine`**, SME **`/flashcards?mode=mine`**. Verified in each course's `src/routes/songs.cards.tsx` redirect and its `korteles.tsx` or `flashcards.tsx` destination. The page can include other course cards too. In a standalone game omit the course action unless the host has a verified target. Open in a new tab on explicit selection and restore notebook focus on return.

## Page design and controls

Open to ruled ivory paper with one ribbon and three labeled thumb tabs. Borrow the pack flap's color for the binding. MH has a navy rule and brass corners. PC uses an indigo rule and woven teal edging with an ochre stitch. Use the current UI font for saved text and a restrained heading face already bundled. Keep texture in the margins. The keepsake pocket looks like a small envelope with the object peeking out above its name. All primary information stays in selectable DOM text.

| Viewport | Layout and acceptance |
|---|---|
| 390×844 | One leaf. Compact labeled tabs above a full-width list. Card detail stacks target, helper, source sentence and labeled speakers. Keep Review above the list when cards are due. |
| 390×664 | Short title, tab picker, scrollable page and pinned Back. At A++ keep the target and its reveal action in one reading sequence. Shrink pocket art before text. Put both grading buttons above the safe-area inset. |
| 844×390 | Index down the left, selected entry or single review card on the right. Wrap long translations. Keep Reveal in the same position after rotation and preserve whether the answer has been shown. |
| 1280×800 | At most 900 px for the spread, centered over the paused-input town. Left page holds the index, right page the selected sentence, keepsake or review. The empty page shows how to save the first card. |

Use the current drawer/menu control on the pad, D-pad/stick to Backpack, A to open/select, B/Esc to step back. Restore focus to the original card, then the pack tool. Tab/Enter reaches each action. Touch and keyboard can finish a review with the sound off. Give speakers distinct names, ≥44 px targets, high-contrast focus and language tags per line. When tabs change, announce leaf and count once. Reduced motion opens the book in its final position, replaces the reveal with an instant text change and keeps cards still while graded.

**Incremental phone budget.** Zero extra town meshes, draw calls, per-frame hit tests and boot media requests. Render at most 30 list entries per batch with an accessible “Show more” control, one expanded detail, one active clip and zero background prefetch. Keep the full card queue in data, not in mounted rows. Reuse existing icon paths and voice cache. Target open-to-text ≤150 ms warm with 500 cards and 1,000 practiced rows. At the same low-quality pose aim for p95 ≤33 ms and ≤2 ms above baseline. Profile on a physical phone. After ten opens, holds, clip requests and notebook listeners should be back to baseline.

# Town hall · A passport for Maple Harbor and Puerto Colibrí

After ordering coffee, walk into town hall and find the café cup pressed into a passport page. Open that impression to see the conversation, the stars you earned and the café lesson in your course. The learner can enjoy the book on the first visit, even with a single stamp.

**Scope.** MH is `glow8/mh` with English Master (EM, `~/fluent-steps`). PC geometry and game data come from `~/claude-work/review2-pc`. Its course profiles are SME (`~/spanish-master-english`) and SM (`~/spanish-master`). Future PC writes belong in a PC implementation worktree. Budget $0. Use existing assets, materials and fonts. Course trees are read-only. Downloads, new paid media and Worker changes are outside the jobs. A change to media access **needs the owner**.
